Overfishing: A Threat to Ocean Balance

Unsustainable fishing reduces stocks, harms habitats, and disrupts food webs. Learn solutions and actions you can take.

Join the Movement

🎣 What Is Overfishing?

When fish are caught faster than they can reproduce, populations decline. Bycatch and destructive gear amplify the damage.

🌐 Global Impacts

Overfishing threatens food security and livelihoods, especially in coastal communities. Ecosystems lose resilience as key species vanish.

🛡️ Solutions

  • Support sustainable seafood and certified fisheries.
  • Protect habitats with marine reserves and better gear.
  • Advocate for science-based catch limits and enforcement.

📊 By the Numbers

A significant share of global fish stocks are overexploited. Recovery is possible with coordinated management and reduced pressure.

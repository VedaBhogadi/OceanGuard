Coral Bleaching: Reefs in Peril

Warming oceans are causing corals to expel the algae they rely on for life, turning reefs white and lifeless. Learn the causes and how to help.

Join the Movement

🌡️ What Causes Coral Bleaching?

Bleaching occurs when corals are stressed by heat, pollution, or acidification and expel the symbiotic algae (zooxanthellae) that give them color and energy.

🐠 Why It Matters

Coral reefs support about 25% of marine life and protect coastlines. Bleached reefs are more vulnerable to disease and can collapse if stress continues.

✅ How You Can Help

  • Support climate action to reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
  • Avoid polluting runoff and choose reef-safe sunscreens.
  • Volunteer with reef monitoring and restoration programs.

📈 Key Facts

Mass bleaching events are increasing in frequency. Without action, many reefs may not recover, threatening biodiversity and food security.

Plastic Pollution: A Global Ocean Crisis

Every minute, one garbage truck’s worth of plastic ends up in our oceans. Let’s explore how it’s harming marine life and what we can do to stop it.

Join the Movement

🌍 The Scale of Plastic Pollution

Over 8 million tons of plastic waste enter our oceans every year. From large debris to invisible microplastics, this pollution is spreading through every corner of the marine ecosystem — from beaches to the deepest trenches.

🐢 Impact on Marine Life

Thousands of marine species ingest or become entangled in plastic waste. Sea turtles mistake plastic bags for jellyfish, and microplastics have been found in fish and seabirds — even in the food we eat.

♻️ What Can We Do?

  • Participate in local beach clean-ups.
  • Refuse single-use plastics — choose reusable bottles and bags.
  • Spread awareness through social media and community projects.
  • Support organizations dedicated to ocean conservation.

📊 The Numbers Speak

By 2050, the ocean could contain more plastic than fish by weight. Only 9% of plastic waste has ever been recycled — the rest ends up polluting landfills, rivers, and seas.

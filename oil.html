Oil Spills: Lasting Scars on Our Oceans

From tankers to rigs, oil spills devastate coastlines and marine life. Explore impacts, response efforts, and prevention.

Join the Movement

🛢️ The Impact of Spills

Oil coats birds and mammals, damages fish gills, and smothers habitats. Cleanup can take years and recovery even longer.

🌊 Ecosystem Recovery

Some species rebound over time, but sensitive habitats like marshes and mangroves can suffer long-term harm without restoration.

🧰 Prevention & Response

  • Stronger regulations and vessel maintenance reduce risk.
  • Rapid response and community reporting limit damage.
  • Support organizations advancing clean energy and safer transport.

🔎 Did You Know?

Small, chronic spills from everyday activity add up and can exceed the impact of single large disasters over time.

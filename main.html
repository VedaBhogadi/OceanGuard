Protecting Our Oceans, Protecting Our Future

Every year, over 8 million tons of plastic waste end up in the ocean — threatening marine life, ecosystems, and human health. OceanGuard empowers individuals to learn, act, and create a cleaner, healthier blue planet.

🧴 Plastic Pollution

Millions of tons of plastic debris choke marine life, damage coral reefs, and enter our food chain through fish and sea salt.

Learn More

🐠 Coral Bleaching

Rising ocean temperatures cause coral reefs to lose their color and vitality, destroying homes for countless marine species.

Learn More

🛢️ Oil Spills

Oil spills suffocate marine species, coat coastlines, and disrupt ecosystems for decades — often with irreversible consequences.

Learn More

🎣 Overfishing

Unsustainable fishing practices deplete fish populations and destroy marine biodiversity, pushing species toward extinction.
